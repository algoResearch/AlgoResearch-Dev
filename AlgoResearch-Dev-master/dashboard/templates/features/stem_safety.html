Stem Cell Research

Safeguard research involving human embryos, pluripotent cells, and chimeric models through advanced protocol routing, ethical review automation, and strict regulatory alignment.

Proposal & Committee Review Framework

Enable researchers to submit stem cell protocols with detailed guidance and automatic routing to institutional review boards. algoResearch ensures that all human-derived or embryo-related work meets review standards before approval.

Pluripotent and Embryonic Stem Cell Oversight

Support research using hESCs, iPSCs, and other human pluripotent stem cells, especially where their application may intersect with embryo development, gametes, or mixed-species entities. Monitor compliance with NIH and institutional ethics policies.

Chimeric & Sensitive Research Classification

algoResearch flags research that introduces human-derived cells into animal models or mixed human-animal entities. Risk scoring, review guidance, and compliance checks support committee decision-making and proper reporting.

Capabilities

  • Submission workflows for all stem cell protocol types
  • Routing for institutional and external committee review
  • Oversight of hESCs derived via IVF or SCNT
  • Support for human iPSC and hPSC research
  • Risk classification for embryo or germ cell creation
  • Mixed human-animal research flagging and documentation
  • Guidance and alerts for NIH policy compliance
  • Exemption workflows for educational or non-integrative use
  • Version tracking and audit logs for sensitive studies
  • Role-based access controls for sensitive datasets
