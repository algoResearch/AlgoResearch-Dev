BioSafety

Protect your people and your program. The algoResearch BioSafety module ensures safe biological research through protocol oversight, hazard tracking, and built-in workflows designed to meet every regulatory expectation.

Biological Protocol Oversight

Centralize all biosafety protocol submissions — from recombinant DNA to BSL-3 studies — with smart form guidance, configurable review cycles, and built-in committee routing. algoResearch ensures no protocol moves forward without safety and compliance checks.

Facility & Equipment Risk Management

Manage biosafety cabinets, human gene transfer settings, and medical surveillance. Automatically track certifications, maintenance schedules, and approvals tied to research spaces, pathogens, and containment levels.

Committee Integration & Hazard Tracking

Align IBC reviews, waste protocols, and surveillance tracking with institutional and federal biosafety standards. algoResearch maintains a living registry of agents, pathogens, cell lines, flow cytometry activities, and more — all tied to real-time researcher and study data.

Capabilities

  • Protocol support for BSL-1 to BSL-3 research
  • Recombinant DNA and human gene transfer workflows
  • Medical surveillance and training validation
  • IBC review routing and committee scheduling
  • Hazard registry: rDNA, pathogens, dual-use agents
  • Biomedical waste and infection control documentation
  • Biosafety cabinet certification and scheduling
  • Flow cytometry and cell sorting safety guidance
  • Automatic alerts for expiring registrations or training
  • Audit-ready logs for all research safety actions
