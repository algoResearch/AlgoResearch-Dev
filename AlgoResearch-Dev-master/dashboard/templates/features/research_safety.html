Research Safety

Protecting people, facilities, and science. Manage biosafety, radiation, chemical, and stem cell research processes through one unified platform that promotes safety, transparency, and institutional compliance.

Research Safety Domains

Bio Safety

Manage protocols involving recombinant DNA, infectious agents, and BSL-classified work. Enable IBC reviews and connect to training and equipment records for institutional assurance.

Radiation Safety

Oversee work with radioactive isotopes or radiation-producing devices. Track exposure, enforce safe practices, and maintain compliance with institutional and federal guidelines.

Chemical Safety

Maintain chemical inventories, handle hazardous materials, and integrate with SDS documentation. Ensure safe handling, storage, and disposal of all lab chemicals.

Stem Cell Safety

Govern the ethical use of stem cell lines, including ESCs and iPSCs. Enable clear protocol oversight and ensure traceability across research use cases and derived materials.

Core Capabilities

  • Smart protocol submission and review workflows by safety domain
  • Facility inspection tracking and audit logging
  • Integrated equipment and location management
  • Controlled substance and inventory oversight
  • Reservation systems for shared safety resources
  • Role-based collaboration between PIs, staff, and safety committees
  • Policy validation against federal and institutional standards
  • Customizable alerts, reports, and compliance dashboards
