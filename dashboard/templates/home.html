  • ✅ Smart Matching: Automatically match opportunities based on eligibility, research interests, and strategic goals.

Build your proposal with precision. Collaborate, complete forms, and ensure compliance — all in one place.

  • 📝 Smart Forms: Complete agency-specific forms including SF-424, RR Budget, and PHS with guided input and field validation.

Whether you're assembling complex, multi-part proposals or quick-turn opportunities, algoRhythm streamlines every detail — ensuring your proposal is complete, accurate, and submission-ready.
