Post-Award

Powerful Tools for Award Management

Post-award administration often involves complex workflows, ranging from budgeting and fund tracking to personnel appointments and compliance reviews. algoResearch centralizes all these workflows to improve efficiency, reduce manual tasks, and support institutional reporting requirements.

Whether managing award closeout, requesting budget modifications, or certifying effort, our system ensures accuracy, audit-readiness, and complete financial transparency — integrated with the rest of your research lifecycle.

Post-Award Features Include:

  • Effort certification with built-in data validation
  • Real-time award and subaward financial tracking
  • Personnel appointment & release time management
  • Spend requests with routing & approvals
  • Grant modifications & budget revisions
  • Close-out workflows with document tracking
  • Residual fund account tracking
  • Progress reporting & deliverables management
  • Audit trail across all financial actions
  • Integrated with proposal and compliance systems
