Welcome to algoResearch

algoResearch is your complete research administration ecosystem—built to unify every phase of research into a single, intelligent, and user-friendly platform. Whether you're managing pre-award submissions, post-award finances, compliance oversight, or operational analytics, algoResearch connects it all.

Designed for institutions that demand flexibility, transparency, and automation, algoResearch helps investigators, administrators, and leadership navigate the research lifecycle without silos, delays, or guesswork. With our end-to-end suite, every task is traceable, every form is smart, and every decision is data-driven.

Why Choose algoResearch?

Seamless Collaboration

  • Unified platform across departments and roles
  • Real-time updates, comments, and versioning
  • Break down communication silos in research

Compliance Without the Complexity

  • Built-in 21 CFR Part 11 and HIPAA compliance
  • Automated disclosures, approvals, and logs
  • Comprehensive audit trails across every action

Actionable Insights

  • Dashboards for study health and funding metrics
  • Forecast trends and resource needs
  • Visualize progress from protocol to publication

Automation at the Core

  • Trigger-based workflows reduce administrative lift
  • Smart forms and reminders ensure timeliness
  • Integrates deeply with grants.gov, eRA, and beyond
