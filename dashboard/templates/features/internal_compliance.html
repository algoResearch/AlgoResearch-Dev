Whistleblower & Internal Compliance

Encrypted Whistleblower Intake Portal

Offers a fully secure and anonymized submission portal for affiliates to report internal failures to implement or enforce antisemitism-related reforms. Supports file uploads, named or anonymous submissions, and multi-tiered routing (e.g., legal, compliance, federal liaison) based on report content. All submissions are time-stamped, stored with immutable metadata, and linked to department-specific compliance files for future audits.

Retaliation Detection & Risk Monitor

Automatically tracks reported retaliation behaviors, including grade drops, professional exclusion, or hostile treatment following a whistleblower report. Monitors patterns using anonymous surveys, course evaluations, and internal feedback forms to surface retaliation that may otherwise go unreported. Cases flagged with high risk of retaliation are escalated for administrative investigation, and protective actions (e.g., mediation, reassignment) are logged in the case record.

Compliance Action Log & Resolution Workflow

Every report triggers a resolution timeline requiring designated administrators to acknowledge, investigate, and respond with action steps. The system logs each phase of the response process, including delays, inaction, or attempts to deflect responsibility — ensuring full traceability. Unresolved reports or patterns of neglect are compiled into a federal risk register and integrated into FICCS's OCR certification package.
