Campus Climate & Antisemitism Audit

Multi-Source Data Aggregator

Continuously pulls structured data from across the FICCS system, including antisemitism reports, conduct violations, disciplinary outcomes, and anonymous climate surveys. Each data point is categorized by incident type, location, department, and resolution status, enabling full-spectrum insight into campus safety and bias trends. This aggregation allows institution’s to demonstrate systemic understanding and federal awareness of where antisemitism is occurring and how it is being addressed.

Risk Visualization

Visual dashboards use color-coded heatmaps to display incident concentration, response times, unresolved cases, and disciplinary trends by school, dormitory, and department. Each unit is assigned a real-time “Compliance Risk Score” based on responsiveness, climate indicators, and repeat violations. Administrators and federal auditors can use this tool to identify at-risk areas and prioritize intervention or oversight.

Trend Tracking & Predictive Analytics

Tracks antisemitism-related activity across semesters and academic years, including spikes around key global events or campus protests. Machine learning models forecast where future climate stress may emerge based on historical data, underreporting patterns, and social media sentiment (if integrated). This forward-looking insight allows the university to proactively prepare, intervene, and demonstrate commitment to prevention—not just response.
