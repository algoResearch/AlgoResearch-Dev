Disclosures

Research & Annual Disclosure Management

Provides a centralized platform for investigators and research staff to submit required annual disclosures and project-specific conflict declarations. Smart forms guide users through relevant financial and relationship disclosures.

Automated Compliance Workflows

Routes submitted disclosures for appropriate review and automatically flags required actions based on thresholds or institutional policy. Built-in alerts and approvals reduce delays and manual oversight burden.

Integrated Risk Monitoring

Cross-references active research with disclosure records to ensure that no activity proceeds without proper transparency and resolution. This protects institutions from noncompliance and supports integrity in all research conduct.

Capabilities

  • NIH ASSIST and Grants.gov integration
  • Real-time error validation and feedback
  • Pre-populated institutional profiles
  • Submission tracking and status updates
  • Collaborative proposal development
