Reporting & Escalation System

Secure, Multi-Channel Report Intake

This intake system allows students, faculty, and staff to file antisemitism reports. Reporters can remain anonymous or self-identify, and choose the nature of the incident (harassment, vandalism, threats, discrimination, etc.), with predefined categories for accurate classification. All entries are end-to-end encrypted, time-stamped, and assigned a unique case ID, ensuring both data integrity and confidentiality for federal Title VI audits.

Automated Case Routing & Escalation

Once submitted, each report is evaluated by severity and type, triggering an automated routing system that assigns the case to the appropriate team: legal, compliance, student discipline, or Title VI officer. High-risk or repeat-pattern incidents trigger escalations with auto-notifications to senior university leadership. Escalation paths are logged with response deadlines and real-time status updates to ensure timely and legally defensible resolutions.

Title VI Tagging & OCR Integration

Each case is scanned for Title VI relevance based on report language, party identity, and context, with flagged cases routed for federal compliance review and statistical analysis. FICCS maintains a library of OCR-compliant data points, ensuring that all logged cases can be formatted into DOE/HHS-ready reports on demand. This supports automatic aggregation of report types, frequency, resolution outcomes, and departmental breakdowns for quarterly Title VI reporting.
