Faculty Screening

Credential & Publication Verification Engine

This tool automatically validates academic credentials, degree authenticity, and employment history for all prospective and current faculty and staff. It cross-references institutional claims with verified databases and flags inconsistencies or false representations. All verification results are logged and attached to personnel files for internal review and federal audit readiness.

Plagiarism & Research Integrity Checker

Upon onboarding and periodically thereafter, faculty publications and dissertations are scanned against global academic databases using advanced plagiarism detection algorithms. Cases of suspected misconduct are routed to an internal integrity board and documented for compliance records. This promotes accountability in scholarship and aligns institution’s with DOE expectations of merit-based, honest academic standards.

Bias & Misconduct Complaint Log

A secure intake system allows students and colleagues to report civil rights-related faculty misconduct, including discriminatory grading, classroom bias, or retaliatory behavior. Reports are tagged by severity and routed for administrative investigation with full anonymization where required. Trends across departments are analyzed to detect hotspots and preempt federal violations before escalation.
