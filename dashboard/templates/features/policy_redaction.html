Policy Audit & Redaction Engine

AI-Powered Policy Scanner

Uses natural language processing (NLP) to scan university policy documents—such as hiring guidelines, conduct codes, and student handbooks—for DEI-aligned language (e.g., “anti-oppression,” “marginalized identities,” “cultural humility”). Each instance is flagged, annotated, and assigned a severity level based on legal risk and ideological content, providing reviewers with redline suggestions. Supports batch uploads and organization by policy type (academic, HR, student affairs) for comprehensive compliance review.

Manual Review & Classification Toolkit

Provides administrators and legal counsel with a collaborative interface to manually assess and classify flagged content as “Remove,” “Revise,” or “Retain with Justification.” Each policy update is logged with user signatures, change notes, and final approval, creating a legally defensible revision history. This tool helps ensure that judgment calls are recorded, reviewed, and standardized across departments.

Audit Trail & Federal Submission Exporter

Maintains version-controlled records of all reviewed and revised policies, including original text, redacted versions, and action notes for each flagged section. Generates DOE-ready compliance packets by department or policy area, including summary metrics on DEI language removed, retained, or revised. Enables institution’s to demonstrate clear and measurable removal of DEI policy infrastructure across every area of operation.
