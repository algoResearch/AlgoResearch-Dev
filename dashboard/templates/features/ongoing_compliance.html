Ongoing Compliance Monitoring

Automated Policy & Proposal Scanner

Continuously scans newly submitted policies, job descriptions, program proposals, and committee charters for DEI-linked terminology or structural patterns using keyword matching and semantic AI. When flagged language is detected (e.g., “inclusive excellence,” “identity-based programming”), the system halts publication or approval until compliance review is complete. This creates a proactive firewall against the reinsertion of DEI practices under rebranded or vague titles.

Departmental Risk

Generates visual compliance dashboards for each academic or administrative unit, showing active DEI risk indicators, flagged content volume, and remediation status. Departments with repeat violations or structural similarities to banned practices are scored higher and flagged for leadership review. This allows centralized oversight while empowering local units to self-correct before triggering federal exposure.

Approval Workflow with Gatekeeping Controls

New initiatives containing flagged keywords or framework similarities are routed through a tiered approval process, involving compliance officers and university legal counsel. Each decision—approve, revise, or reject—is logged with justification and timestamped for internal audit or external submission. This workflow ensures no DEI-aligned content is revived without formal scrutiny and creates accountability at every step of university governance.
