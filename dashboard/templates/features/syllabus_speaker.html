Ideological Framework Classifier

This tool uses machine learning and keyword tagging to analyze course syllabi and identify the dominant theoretical or ideological frameworks in assigned readings (e.g., critical theory, classical liberalism, postcolonialism). It categorizes readings by perspective and calculates a balance score for each syllabus or course cluster. This allows administrators to detect intellectual homogeneity and recommend corrective inclusion of underrepresented perspectives.

Ideological Tagging System

Faculty are required to log invited speakers along with their institutional affiliations, fields, and published ideological positions (where publicly available). The system scores ideological diversity across speaker series and events, flagging when panels or lecture programs reflect narrow ideological representation. These scores are aggregated by department to evaluate public intellectual exposure and invite diversity.

Departmental Balance Heatmap

All data from syllabi and speaker logs are visualized on a heatmap that displays the concentration of ideological frameworks by department or academic unit. Departments are color-coded from high diversity to high concentration, with drill-downs available to course and faculty level. The heatmap provides leadership and auditors with a clear view of intellectual balance trends across the institution.
