Radiation Safety

Ensure institutional safety and federal compliance in all radiation-related research. From dosimetry to waste disposal, algoResearch equips institutions to monitor, approve, and document safe practices at every level.

Protocol Review & Committee Integration

Enable researchers to submit and update radiation use protocols with smart routing to Radiation Safety Committees. Built-in review logic ensures compliance with NRC, FDA, OSHA, and internal policy — from initial use to close-out.

Dosimetry, Purchasing & Surveys

Manage radiation badges, staff exposure tracking, procurement approvals, and facility surveys in a centralized platform. algoResearch automation ensures timely renewals, training checks, and dosage recordkeeping.

X-Ray, Laser & Radioactive Waste Management

Support oversight of regulated devices and safe disposal of radioactive materials. Whether reviewing new laser equipment or managing waste from sealed sources, our system tracks every action for audit-readiness and safety assurance.

  • Compliance workflows tied to NRC, FDA, and OSHA standards
  • Dosimetry badge issuance and exposure tracking
  • Radiation equipment and isotope purchasing approvals
  • Facility radiation surveys and inspection tracking
  • Laser and X-ray safety program documentation
  • Radioactive waste management and disposal workflows
  • Training verification for radiation handlers
  • Real-time dashboards for compliance and renewal status
  • Audit logs and automated reminders
