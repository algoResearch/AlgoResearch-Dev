Chemical Safety

Maintain safe environments for chemical research with smart protocol tracking, committee review workflows, and compliance automation that aligns with OSHA, institutional policy, and hazard-specific controls.

Protocol Submission & Committee Review

algoResearch supports the full lifecycle of chemical safety protocols, enabling researchers to submit proposed work and committees to review based on agent type, hazard level, and lab setup — reducing risks from the start.

Hazard Identification & Personal Protective Oversight

Manage chemical inventory, hazard classifications, and ensure safety through automated validation of respiratory protection, PPE use, and lab readiness. Smart alerts notify stakeholders of restricted substances or hazard thresholds.

Exposure Monitoring & Waste Management

Monitor exposure to chemicals and physical agents through automated reporting and scheduling. algoResearch tracks disposal of hazardous waste, responds to odor/IAQ complaints, and ensures compliance with OSHA and EPA policies.

Capabilities

  • Protocol routing for chemical research and hazard review
  • Hazard Communication compliance tracking
  • Restricted/particularly hazardous substances flagging
  • Inventory and chemical registration monitoring
  • Respiratory protection and PPE validation workflows
  • Silica and airborne contaminant exposure documentation
  • Chemical waste disposal logging and oversight
  • Indoor air quality and odor complaint management
  • Lab inspection and readiness validation
  • OSHA standard alignment and automated training checks
