Admissions & Disciplinary Transparency Portal

Anonymized International Admissions

Publishes summary statistics of international student admissions, including number of applicants, offers, and rejections by nationality, region, visa type, and program. Academic indicators such as GPA bands, standardized test scores, and language proficiency are included in aggregate to demonstrate nondiscriminatory, merit-based admissions practices. Deans of Admissions digitally sign off each cycle to certify policy compliance and ensure transparency with federal standards.

Disciplinary Action Disclosure Index

Compiles and displays anonymized disciplinary records of international students, including incident types, resolution outcomes, and sanctions imposed (e.g., suspension, warning, expulsion). Ensures public documentation of enforcement consistency and shows that international students are held to the same standards as domestic peers. The data can be filtered by academic unit and updated each semester to maintain federal transparency obligations.

Federal Reporting & Certification

Automatically generates downloadable packets for DOE, DHS, and OCR that include admission and discipline summaries, signed certifications, and audit logs. Ensures that each report meets formatting, redaction, and verification requirements with timestamped sign-offs by legal, admissions, and compliance officers. This provides an end-to-end record proving an institution’s transparency and equal treatment of international applicants and students.
