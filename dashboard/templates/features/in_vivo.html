In Vivo

Design. Execute. Analyze. Track the full lifecycle of research studies across organizations, automate data capture, and ensure accuracy and compliance in every phase.

Standardized & Repeatable Protocols

Build and reuse structured experimental protocols with clearly defined phases, procedures, and measurement presets. Reduce variability and accelerate research by creating a template-based approach to study design.

Connected Data Collection

Capture study data in real time from both manual entry and connected instruments. Visualize results immediately, generate exports, and ensure traceability across all timepoints and observations.

Built-In Randomization & Compliance Checks

Assign subjects randomly to arms or conditions using built-in algorithms that eliminate bias. Ensure all animal welfare, ethical, and procedural standards are automatically monitored throughout the study lifecycle.

Core Capabilities

  • Reusable experimental design templates
  • Preset scheduling for study phases and procedures
  • Real-time data capture via connected instruments
  • Configurable outcome measurements and endpoints
  • Visual dashboards for study progress and completion
  • Subject randomization and masking
  • Audit trail of compliance interactions
  • Exportable results and visualizations for review
