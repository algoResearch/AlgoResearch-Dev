Student Conduct & Discipline

Violation Intake & Classification Workflow

The system logs reported conduct violations such as harassment, unauthorized encampments, classroom disruptions, and assault, tagging each case by category, severity, and policy breached. It auto-generates a structured case file with time-stamped evidence uploads (videos, witness statements, ID scans) and assigns the case to a university official based on role and jurisdiction. Standardized intake ensures that every reported infraction follows a common procedural baseline across departments, improving fairness and audit consistency.

Due Process & Sanctions

Manages all stages of investigation: notice to accused, hearing scheduling, sanction decision, and appeal processing, with automated reminders and step logging. Pre-loaded sanction guidelines ensure proportional penalties based on violation type (e.g., suspension, loss of student group privileges, campus ban), and all overrides require documented justification. The system tracks the final outcome, appeal result, and execution of disciplinary action, allowing compliance reviewers to monitor equity and enforcement consistency over time.

Federal & Stakeholder Reporting Toolkit

All conduct records are formatted into structured outputs for OCR and internal reviews, with case-level and aggregate analytics for categories like antisemitic incidents, protest violations, or academic disruptions. The toolkit includes templates for quarterly and annual reports for internal stakeholders. FERPA-protected redaction tools allow institution’s to share outcomes publicly (where permissible) without breaching student privacy, enhancing institutional transparency.
