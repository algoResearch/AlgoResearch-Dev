Civil Rights Policy Violation Monitor

Automated Language Scanner

This engine continuously scans job descriptions, admissions criteria, internal policy documents, and promotional materials for race-, sex-, or identity-based preferences or coded language (e.g., “underrepresented identity requirement”). It uses natural language processing (NLP) and keyword classification to detect violations aligned with Title VI and VII restrictions. When violations are found, the document is flagged, and revision is required before approval.

Pre-Posting Approval Workflow

All hiring or admissions announcements must pass through a compliance pre-check before they are published internally or externally. The system blocks release of any material containing flagged DEI-style criteria or unlawful demographic filters until a clean version is submitted. Each cleared posting is assigned a unique compliance ID that can be referenced in audits and reporting.

Violation Escalation Queue

If a violation is detected and not resolved within a defined timeframe, the system escalates the case to legal/compliance officers and notifies departmental leadership. A log of unresolved or repeat violations is maintained and reviewed during quarterly audits. This mechanism ensures systemic issues are addressed proactively and departments remain accountable for civil rights adherence.
