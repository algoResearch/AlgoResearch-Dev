Vivarium

Define environments, track animal health, and ensure welfare. The operational core of your animal facility—rooms, racks, cages, protocols, and life histories, all managed in one place.

Rooms, Racks & Cages — Digitally Defined

Digitally define and manage physical housing layouts across your institution. From room-level tracking to individual cages, Vivarium enables precision in mapping, maintenance, and operational oversight.

Individual Animal Management

Assign RFID tags or barcodes to track animals uniquely. Monitor life history, health records, procedures, and welfare in real time. Build robust animal profiles tied to protocols and timelines.

Track Census, Welfare & Protocols

Maintain accurate census logs, track animal welfare events, and link housing to active protocols. Capture every interaction with audit-ready logs that align with IACUC and regulatory requirements.

Core Capabilities

  • Digital maps of rooms, racks, and cages
  • RFID/barcode-based individual animal tracking
  • Customizable health and procedure logs
  • Census and occupancy reporting
  • Protocol-based housing and traceability
  • Alerts for welfare thresholds and environmental checks
  • Integrated dashboards and audit trails
  • Role-based access for care staff, investigators, and compliance officers
