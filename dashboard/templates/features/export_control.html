Export Control

Manage export compliance and foreign collaboration risks with advanced monitoring, classification tools, and license tracking — all in one secure research administration hub.

Technology Control & Licensing

Classify technologies, identify export restrictions, assess licensing needs, and generate Technology Control Plans (TCPs). algoResearch automates exemption workflows and license requests tied to embargoed countries and export-controlled research.

Vetting & Screening Tools

Conduct restricted party screening, track travel to sanctioned countries, monitor foreign national access, and review international shipments. Integrates with third-party tools and research administration systems for centralized oversight.

Policy, Records & Compliance Monitoring

Ensure audit-readiness with detailed logs, exemption certificates, and export license records. algoResearch monitors training completion, provides real-time dashboards, and supports policy enforcement with automated reminders.
