DEI Asset Identification

& Registry System

DEI Asset Discovery & Classification

Automatically scans university data sources—such as HR titles, program catalogs, organizational charts, and budget allocations—to detect and classify any program, office, position, or funding tied to DEI concepts. Using a curated library of keywords (“equity,” “inclusion,” “anti-racism,” etc.), the system assigns each entry a DEI relevance score and categorizes it as a policy, role, program, or funding stream. This initiates a transparent, searchable registry where each identified asset becomes an object for status tracking, audit documentation, and compliance review.

Status Tagging & Closed-Loop Documentation

Allows authorized users (e.g., deans or compliance officers) to assign one of three statuses to each asset—“Active,” “Eliminated,” or “Repurposed”—and upload documentation (e.g., termination memos, updated org charts, funding reallocation records). Every change is timestamped and versioned, maintaining an audit trail that shows not only when a DEI asset was closed or altered, but also how compliance obligations were satisfied. This creates legally defensible proof that an institution systematically and permanently dismantled each identified DEI entity.

DEI Asset Impact & Compliance Report

Compiles and summarizes registry data into an exportable audit package—including counts of eliminated assets, status changes, and associated documentation—and formats it into DOE-compliant report templates. Offers filters by asset type, department, or date, enabling institution’s to provide federal reviewers with both a high-level overview and detailed, drill-down capability. This subfeature ensures rapid, transparent reporting and forms the basis of public-facing compliance dashboard on DEI elimination.
