Merit Based Decisions

Rubric-Based Evaluation Templates

Each department configures standardized scorecards for admissions and hiring based on pre-approved academic and professional criteria (e.g., GPA, research output, teaching evaluations). The system ensures demographic fields (race, sex, national origin) are masked during evaluation to prevent implicit bias. These templates are version-controlled and locked for audit once scores are submitted, ensuring the integrity of the decision process.

Decision Justification Logger

Every time a candidate is selected, admissions or hiring committee members must submit a written rationale tied to the rubric scores. The system automatically attaches these statements to each candidate’s file, timestamped and stored in a non-editable audit trail. This protects against arbitrary or identity-based selections and provides defensible evidence during federal audits.

Anomaly Detection & Compliance Alerts

The engine runs algorithms to flag decisions that deviate significantly from scoring patterns — such as selecting a candidate with lower scores or skipping over higher-ranked individuals. When an anomaly is detected, the system prompts reviewers to input additional justification or escalate the case for compliance review. This feature ensures consistent enforcement of merit-based criteria and prevents covert identity-preference practices.
