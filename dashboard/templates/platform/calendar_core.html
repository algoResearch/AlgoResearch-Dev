Calendar

More than just scheduling. Calendar connects people, tasks, and projects in real time — built for collaboration, optimized for oversight, and designed for research environments.

Live Collaboration Through Messaging

Calendar works in real time. As conversations unfold in Messaging, meetings and events can be created instantly through WebSocket-powered updates — keeping teams aligned without needing to switch tools.

Schedule What Matters — Not Just Meetings

From protocol milestones to grant reviews and compliance checks, Calendar supports more than just time slots. Events are linked to tasks, messages, and modules across the platform for true contextual scheduling.

Visibility for Admins and Managers

See individual or team availability, task loads, and time conflicts at a glance. Calendar gives leadership tools to schedule effectively and ensure compliance events are never missed.

Core Capabilities

  • WebSocket-powered real-time updates
  • Schedule tasks, meetings, and compliance events
  • Convert messages into events automatically
  • Task- and protocol-aware scheduling
  • Visibility into team availability and task load
  • Admin scheduling controls and approvals
  • Notifications synced across platform
  • Exportable and external calendar sync-ready
  • Fully permission-aware and audit-logged
