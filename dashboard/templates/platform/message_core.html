Messaging

Secure, compliant, and built for research. Connect investigators, coordinators, and committees with encrypted, role-aware messaging that powers workflows across the platform.

Built for Research-Grade Security

All communications are end-to-end encrypted and compliant with institutional, state, and federal guidelines. Access is role-based and governed by the platform's permission model — ensuring the right people, and only the right people, see protected data.

Research Groups, Committees & Teams — Connected

Collaborate across labs, committees, grants, and studies. Up to 300 users can work together in a single encrypted conversation with threads, file sharing, and shared context tied to protocols or submissions.

Integrated with Calendar, Tasks & Workflows

Turn a message into a task, schedule a meeting, or trigger a workflow. Messaging is fully integrated with algoResearch's task and calendar systems — allowing conversations to become action instantly.
