algoResearch

Smarter Research Management from Bench to Insight

The Research suite unifies all facets of laboratory operations, data capture, compliance, and collaboration under one platform. Whether you’re managing in vivo studies, coordinating vivarium logistics, or generating scientific insights, our tools streamline the process and reduce the administrative burden that slows innovation.

Designed with scientists, research coordinators, and compliance officers in mind, algoResearch ensures that your team remains focused on discovery — not documentation. Every feature is built for traceability, integrity, and repeatability, aligning with regulatory standards while accelerating the research lifecycle.

Key Capabilities Include:

  • In Vivo Study Management: Design, track, and record experiments with complete auditability
  • Vivarium Oversight: Coordinate room scheduling, animal assignments, and daily care
  • Intelligent Scheduler: Allocate equipment and staff efficiently across protocols
  • Regulatory Readiness: Automatically align with IACUC, AAALAC, and institutional policies
  • Live Status Dashboards: Monitor experimental progress and facility load in real time
  • Data Traceability: Link observations, treatments, and results directly to subjects
  • Workflow Automation: Reduce manual entries with smart protocol templates
  • Role-based Access: Ensure secure and appropriate control over sensitive information
  • Insight Reports: Surface performance trends, resource usage, and outcomes
  • Integrated Collaboration: Keep lab teams, managers, and compliance aligned
