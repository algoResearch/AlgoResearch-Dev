Compliance

Explore Compliance Tools

The algoResearch Compliance suite ensures that every aspect of your research program — from human subjects to biohazards — operates within the guardrails of institutional, state, and federal policy.

By combining intelligent workflows, transparent documentation, and configurable review logic, our tools reduce administrative overhead while strengthening audit-readiness, data integrity, and participant safety.

Compliance Features Include:

  • Protocol Submission: Smart, auto-validating submission tools for all compliance types
  • Continuing Reviews: Automated notifications and configurable review cycles
  • Amendments: Track changes across protocol history with full audit visibility
  • Integrated Committee Portals: Review workflows built for IACUC, IRB, and IBC processes
  • Disclosures Management: Handle COI, COC, and financial disclosures across studies
  • Training Validations: Auto-check required certifications before routing
  • Hazard Registry: Built-in tracking of rDNA, select agents, and biohazards
  • Multi-PI Support: Route by role and responsibility
  • Version Control: Keep protocol documents aligned across updates
  • Institutional Policy Mapping: Customize workflows based on internal policy variations
