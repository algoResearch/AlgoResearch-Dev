Federally Integrated Civil Compliance System

Federal Compliance

The Federally Integrated Civil Compliance System (FICCS) is a unified software platform designed to ensure full institutional compliance with federal mandates following findings from the Department of Education and related agencies. It includes five core modules that address civil rights and discrimination (Title VI/VII), viewpoint diversity and ideological balance, antisemitism and student discipline, DEI elimination, and international student oversight. Each module centralizes policy enforcement, incident tracking, audit generation, and federal reporting. FICCS uses automation, real-time alerts, and data visualization to enforce structural reform while protecting academic freedom and legal integrity. The system enables institutions to document, sustain, and transparently report reforms through 2028 and beyond. By integrating all compliance demands into one platform, FICCS transforms federal accountability from a burden into a durable operational standard.
